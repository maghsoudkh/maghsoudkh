### A. Maghsoud

M.Sc. student in Electronic Engineering at the University of Padova. I work on III-N semiconductor devices made from GaN and InGaN. I model devices in Synopsys Sentaurus TCAD, measure them in the lab and fit the measurements to device models to extract physical parameters.

I am looking for a paid Master's thesis on GaN or optoelectronic devices from spring 2027. Website: [maghsoudkh.github.io](https://maghsoudkh.github.io)

#### Projects

- [Semiconductor device characterization](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microelectronics-device-characterization). Sentaurus simulations and lab measurements of PN, Schottky and MOS devices in Si, SiC and GaAs. Diode I–V curves at six temperatures give a silicon bandgap of 1.152 eV. The C–V curve of a MOS capacitor gives its oxide thickness and substrate doping.
- [Analog CMOS integrated circuit design](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/analog-ic-design-cmos). Four circuits in a 130 nm process, designed in Cadence Virtuoso and checked over process corners and with Monte Carlo runs: current mirrors, a fully differential OTA with a 1.01 GHz gain-bandwidth product, a two-stage feedback amplifier and an 800 mV bandgap reference.
- [Waveguides, couplers and matching networks](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microwave-engineering). Modal analysis of a rectangular waveguide, a microstrip branch-line coupler and impedance-matching networks, in MATLAB and CST.
- [B.Sc. thesis on deep learning accelerators](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/bsc-thesis-deep-learning-accelerators). GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs and ASICs compared by throughput, latency, energy efficiency and cost.

Two projects are in progress and stay private until the course ends: a Sentaurus model of an InGaN/GaN LED with three quantum wells, and spectral measurements of LEDs and a laser diode against current and temperature.
