### Amirkhashayar Maghsoud

I am an M.Sc. student in Electronic Engineering at the University of Padova. I study semiconductor devices, mainly III-N devices made from GaN and InGaN. I model devices in Synopsys Sentaurus TCAD, measure them in the lab, and fit the measurements to device models to extract physical parameters.

#### Projects

- [Semiconductor device characterization](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microelectronics-device-characterization). Sentaurus simulations and lab measurements of PN, Schottky and MOS devices in Si, SiC and GaAs. I extracted a silicon bandgap of 1.152 eV from diode I–V curves at six temperatures, and the oxide thickness and substrate doping of a MOS capacitor from its C–V curve.
- [Analog CMOS IC design](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/analog-ic-design-cmos). Four circuits in a 130 nm process, designed in Cadence Virtuoso and checked with process corners and Monte Carlo: current mirrors, a fully differential OTA with a 1 GHz gain-bandwidth product, a two-stage feedback amplifier and a 0.8 V bandgap reference.
- [Microwave devices](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microwave-engineering). Modal analysis of a rectangular waveguide and a modal filter for it, a microstrip branch-line coupler, and impedance-matching networks, in MATLAB and CST.

I am now modelling an InGaN/GaN LED with three quantum wells in Sentaurus, and measuring LEDs and laser diodes at different temperatures and currents. That work is coursework in progress and not public yet.
