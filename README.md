### Amirkhashayar Maghsoud

M.Sc. student in Electronic Engineering at the University of Padova, Italy.

I work on semiconductor devices. I simulate them in Synopsys Sentaurus TCAD, characterize them in the lab, and extract physical parameters by fitting the measurements to device models. My main interest is III-N electronic and optoelectronic devices based on GaN and InGaN.

#### Selected projects

| Project | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Semiconductor device characterization](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microelectronics-device-characterization) | Sentaurus TCAD and lab measurements of PN, Schottky and MOS devices in Si, SiC and GaAs. The silicon bandgap extracted from I–V at several temperatures is 1.152 eV, and C–V gives t<sub>ox</sub> and N<sub>A</sub> of a MOS capacitor. |
| [Analog CMOS IC design](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/analog-ic-design-cmos) | Current mirrors, a 1 GHz fully differential OTA, a two-stage feedback amplifier and a low-voltage bandgap reference in 130 nm CMOS, designed in Cadence Virtuoso and checked with corners and Monte Carlo. |
| [Microwave devices](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microwave-engineering) | Waveguide modal analysis and a modal filter, a microstrip branch-line coupler, and impedance-matching networks, in MATLAB and CST. |

I am also working on coursework that is not public yet: a Sentaurus model of an InGaN/GaN triple-quantum-well LED with an AlGaN electron-blocking layer, and lab measurements of LEDs and laser diodes at different temperatures and currents.
