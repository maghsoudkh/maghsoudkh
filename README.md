### Amirkhashayar Maghsoud

M.Sc. student in **Electronic Engineering** at the **University of Padova**, Italy.

I work on **semiconductor devices**: TCAD simulation in Synopsys Sentaurus, electrical characterization in the lab, and extracting physical parameters by comparing measurements with device models. I am most interested in **III-N (GaN/InGaN) electronic and optoelectronic devices**.

#### Selected projects

| Project | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [**Semiconductor device characterization**](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microelectronics-device-characterization) | Sentaurus TCAD and lab measurements of PN, Schottky (Si, SiC, GaAs) and MOS devices. Silicon bandgap extracted from I–V(T) (1.152 eV); t<sub>ox</sub> and N<sub>A</sub> extracted from C–V. |
| [**Analog CMOS IC design**](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/analog-ic-design-cmos) | Current mirrors, a 1 GHz fully differential OTA, a two-stage feedback amplifier and a low-voltage bandgap reference in 130 nm CMOS (Cadence Virtuoso), verified with corners and Monte Carlo. |
| [**Microwave devices**](https://github.com/maghsoudkh/microwave-engineering) | Waveguide modal analysis and modal filter, a microstrip branch-line coupler and impedance-matching networks (MATLAB and CST). |

Also in progress (coursework, not public): TCAD simulation of an InGaN/GaN triple-quantum-well LED with an AlGaN electron-blocking layer, and laboratory characterization of LEDs and laser diodes against temperature and current.
